# Multisensory Space

## Inspiration

The time I felt most at peace with the world was in the temperate rainforest of the Pacific Northwest, when I knelt and hugged a clump of moss that was bigger than I was. The world felt so vast, and yet, friendly. I was held, fully, for a moment by the spongy softness of the moss, the aroma of the forest floor, and the quiet rustling of the trees.

I want to recreate this experience of humility, natural connection, and peace for my micro-environment. My inspiration is combining the calming aesthetics of a traditional Japanese tea house with the hollow of a mossy tree stump, and creating the feeling of being a small creature in a big world.

If they choose to crawl inside, the Wanderer would find themselves in a rounded chamber, around 2.5m in diameter, and 4m in height. The ceiling is entirely composed of a rounded, slightly-domed skylight. The walls are dark wood in a minimally refined texture, preserving natural fluctuations in thickness and shape, with some gentle sanding for smoothness. Most of the floors pace is occupied by a pile of plump, jumbo cushions covered in fabrics which emulate the colors and textures of various species of mosses, with the texture enlarged in proportion with the scale of the cushions.

![Storyboard image](./storyboard-2.jpg)

If they choose to touch the mosses, the textures will be very soft. The fern frond foliage of fern mosses will be crocheted in yarn to a scale which you can run your fingers through. Star mosses will be rendered in florets of plush leaflets the size of your hands. The cushions are filled with memory foam, such that you can sink into them, and they will slowly morph back into their original shape when you relieve pressure.



The Wanderer will also notice (through aroma and sight), a plate of cookies and tray with cups, sitting on a low (0.3m), shallow (0.5m)curved counter that undulates around part of the border of the space.

The cups are in the shape of lichens, (specifically the fruiting structures of *Cladonia pyxidata*, the Pebbled Pixie Cup Lichen.)

![Storyboard image](./storyboard-3.jpg)

Nearby the cups, there is a spigot emerging from the wall with a wide, curved lever underneath, to signify and afford pressing a cup underneath. The spigot will dispense steaming conifer tea, which warms the cup and has a citrusy, woodsy aroma.

The cookies are a shortbread flavored with powdered pine needles. They are buttery, lightly sweetened, and the pine needles give them a bright, lemony, forest flavor.

## Sensory Prototypes
![Sensory Prototype image](./sensory-prototype.jpg)

I modeled the ceramic plate and cup after lichens. The cookies are a store-bought placeholder, but I hope to bake some real pine-needle shortbread and see how it turns out! I also hope to brew a forest-y tea and see if the aroma is calming, if the heat transfers pleasantly from the cup to the hand, and if the organically-shaped rim of the cup is pleasant to drink from.
