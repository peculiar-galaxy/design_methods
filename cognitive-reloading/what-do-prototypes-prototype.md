# What do Prototypes Prototype?

With Figma, we are making a prototype that primarily mocks up the 'role' of our artifact - the "cognative reloading" from doggedly following a recipe, to being able to make a pizza from scratch without needing a recipe. Some 'look n feel' may also be explored.
